geometor.press.project_medallions.phyllotaxis¶

Project Medallion: Phyllotaxis — Botanical Golden-Angle Growth.

Sacred geometric emblem for geometor/phyllotaxis framed within the unified R = 68 graduated astrolabe seal:

  1. Exact Vogel Sunflower Lattice (``n = 1..55``): Every floret sits at exact golden-angle polar coordinates theta_n = n * 360 / phi^2 (137.507764... deg) and Fermat radius r_n = c * sqrt(n), scaled to fill the botanical receptacle R_rec = 60 with 55 crisp non-overlapping circular florets.

  2. Opposing Fibonacci Parastichy Spirals (``8``-Fold & ``13``-Fold): Exact 3-point circular arcs (zero Beziers) thread every consecutive floret pair (n, n + 13) in crisp detail clockwise arms and (n, n + 8) in luminous golden counter-clockwise arms, illuminating the dual Fibonacci phyllotactic lattice.

  3. Fibonacci Floret Jewels & Golden-Angle Wedge: Florets indexed by the Fibonacci sequence (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34, 55) are crowned with radiant golden rings and filled cores, anchored by the central golden-angle divergence wedge 137.508 deg.
