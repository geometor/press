Build the 4-layer geometric portrait of Apollonius of Perga.

Calibrated to the 1537 Venice woodcut bust: high domed forehead with contemplation furrows, curly framing hair, three-quarter right-turned gaze on y = -27, long tapered philosopher’s beard crafted with Euclid III.12 tangent wave arcs, draped scholar’s robe, and the foreground geometric diagram disc & dividers.
