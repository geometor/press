geometor.press.portrait_studies.apollonius.build_apollonius_elements(wave_angle_deg: float = 30.0) → dict[str, list[dict[str, Any]]]¶

Build the 4-layer geometric portrait of Apollonius of Perga.

Calibrated to the 1537 Venice woodcut bust: high domed forehead with contemplation furrows, curly framing hair, three-quarter right-turned gaze on y = -27, long tapered philosopher’s beard crafted with Euclid III.12 tangent wave arcs, draped scholar’s robe, and the foreground geometric diagram disc & dividers. Margin motifs display an exact Apollonian gasket (left) and coaxal Circles of Apollonius (right).
