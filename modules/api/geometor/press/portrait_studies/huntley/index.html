geometor.press.portrait_studies.huntley¶

Study 45: H. E. Huntley — Pure Sacred Geometric Emblem Medallion.

Celebrates H. E. Huntley’s The Divine Proportion: A Study in Mathematical Beauty (1970), uniting two of Huntley’s signature golden constructions across a shared diameter AB inside the unified R = 68 graduated astrolabe seal:

  1. Upper Semicircle — ``3-4-5`` Right-Triangle Angle-Bisector Golden Section: Inscribed on horizontal diameter AB = 5u, right triangle ABC (AC = 3u, BC = 4u, AB = 5u) has internal angle bisector of angle A meeting leg BC at D (CD = 1.5u, DB = 2.5u). The golden circle (D, r = DC) is simultaneously tangent to leg AC at C and hypotenuse AB at T, cutting ray AD at P and Q in exact golden proportion: |AQ|/|AC| = |AC|/|AP| = |AQ|/|PQ| = |PQ|/|AP| = phi (verified < 1e-12).

  2. Lower Semicircle — Golden Gnomon, Golden Triangle (``36-72-72``), & ``G1``-Smooth *Spira Mirabilis*: Sharing base AB, the obtuse Golden Gnomon ABW (108-36-36) subdivides at golden-section point K into a 36-72-72 Golden Triangle BKW and recursive golden triangles whose seven 108 deg circular arcs connect with exact G1 tangency into the luminous golden spiral pole.
