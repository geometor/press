Inaugurating GEOMETOR Press: Master Shelf, Brand Architecture & Studios¶

Established geometor/press (v0.1.0) as the dedicated headquarters for GEOMETOR Press, consolidating our workspace-wide publishing census, brand identity, cover/spine design studies, and interactive object workbenches.

Highlights¶

  • 48-Repository Shelf Manifest (resources/shelf.yaml & resources/shelf.json): Cataloged 44+ distinct book works, 71,900+ vector SVGs, and 2,377 archival folios across four readiness stages, organized into five mineral-pigment Spine Series (Fontes Geometrici, Bibliotheca Noetica, Synthesis & Constructio, Morphogenesis & Natura, and Instrumenta & Machina).

  • 12 Visual Concept Plates (docsrc/_static/lookbook/): Archived comparative studies across Round 1 classical framed bindings (Hoenen, Euclid, Polychrome Byrne, Divine Constructions), Round 2 alternate design philosophies (Full-Bleed Projective Loci, \(1:\phi\) Split-Band, Ratdolt 1482 Letterpress, Raw Linen Blind-Deboss), matched 5-series spines, collector card decks, greeting cards, and 24″ × 36″ museum broadsides.

  • Standalone Parametric SVG Studios (demos/cover_studio.html & demos/cards_posters_studio.html): Built zero-CDN interactive studios supporting live binding switches, \(\phi\) canon overlays, 3D-flipping study cards, and instant vector .svg export.

  • Typed Python 3.13 Package & CLI (geometor.press): Implemented load_shelf, scan_workspace, and the press CLI (summary, shelf, products, census).
