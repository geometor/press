==============================
Brand, Series & Print Strategy
==============================

Imprint Architecture
--------------------

- **Public Imprint**: **GEOMETOR Press** (*"Exploring the Architecture of All That Is — Exact Symbolic & Classical Editions"*), anchored at ``geometor.com`` with the *Vesica-to-* :math:`\phi` construction as its universal printer's mark and spine colophon.
- **Typesetting & Packaging Engine**: ``photon-platform/publish`` (*"Typeset & Compiled with PHOTON Publish"*), keeping historical/mathematical books under **GEOMETOR Press** while allowing sibling imprints (such as **phi ARCHITECT Press** for *GOR-9*) to share the exact same production toolchain.

The Five Mineral Spine Series
-----------------------------

To unify 40+ works ranging from 380 BCE Athens to modern Python/SymPy theorem provers—without losing instant subject legibility on a bookshelf—every volume belongs to one of five mineral-pigment series sharing aligned spine medallions and colophon datums:

   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 22 16 62

   * - Series
     - Mineral Pigment
     - Editorial Scope & Representative Works
   * - **I. Fontes Geometrici**
     - Warm Ochre / Gold (``#b8860b``)
     - Classical, Medieval & Renaissance primary geometric treatises: *Euclid*, *Archimedes*, *Apollonius*, *Hero*, *Nicomachus*, *Menelaus*, *Ptolemy*, *Pappus*, *Fibonacci*, *Pacioli*, *Dürer*, *Kepler*, *Ceva*, *Oliver Byrne*.
   * - **II. Bibliotheca Noetica**
     - Venetian Rubric Red (``#8b3a3a``)
     - Philosophical, epistemological & axiomatic foundations of geometry: *Plato*, *Aristotle*, *Proclus*, *Aquinas*, *Hoenen*, *von Staudt*, *Klein*, *Hilbert*, *Poincaré*, *Bertrand Russell*.
   * - **III. Synthesis & Constructio**
     - Prussian Blueprint Blue (``#2b5b84``)
     - Modern synthetic, projective & computable geometry: *Elements 2.0 (G.1–G.601)*, *Divine Constructions*, *ETC Triangle Centers*, *Casey*, *Petersen*, *Lemoine*, *Altshiller-Court*, *Feuerbach*, *Wildberger*, *Knott*, *Seelig Fundamentals*.
   * - **IV. Morphogenesis & Natura**
     - Botanical Viridian (``#2e6f40``)

     - Spectral Violet (``#5a4fcf``)
     - Symbolic engines, formal grammars & machine reasoning: *GEOMETOR Model Handbook*, *GEOMETOR Codes (11 Formal Languages)*, *Explorer*, *Seer Observatory*.

Three Standard Binding & Trim Canons
------------------------------------

1. **Trade Scholarly Octavo** (``6″ × 9″``, Aldine Warm Cotton Rag cover + colored mineral spine): Clean accessible editions for single-language monographs, translations, and study companions.
2. **Bilingual Facing-Page Royal Octavo** (``7″ × 10″``): Synchronized *verso* source language (Greek, Latin, German, French, Italian) opposite *recto* English translation with shared paragraph/Bekker/Stephanus/Friedlein apparatus.
3. **Collector Fine Quarto** (``8″ × 10″`` or ``8.5″ × 11″``, Nocturnal Obsidian Cloth + Gold Foil or Full-Cloth): Diagram-first flagship editions (*Euclid*, *Polychrome Byrne*, *Pacioli & Leonardo*, *Dürer*, *ETC Selected Atlas*).

Market Benchmarks & Two-Tier Fulfillment Strategy
-------------------------------------------------

Studying two landmark mathematical design publishers clarifies both the creative opportunity and the operational discipline required:

- **Nicholas Rougeux** (``c82.net`` — *Byrne's Euclid*, * Werner's Nomenclature*, fine-art data posters): Proves strong organic demand when interactive web codices pair directly with archival print-on-demand posters and books—with zero warehouse risk.
- **Kronecker Wallis** (Kickstarter editions of *Euclid*, *Newton*, *Archimedes*, *Hilbert*): Proves huge global appetite for minimalist geometric redesigns of classical science, while demonstrating the danger of managing bespoke international crowdfunding logistics manually.

**GEOMETOR Press Fulfillment Model**:

1. **Tier 1 — Always-In-Print Catalog (Zero Inventory)**: Automated PDF/X-1a interiors + parametric wrap covers routed to **IngramSpark / Lulu Direct** (global bookstore & direct storefront fulfillment) and **Prodigi / Printful** archival museum cotton rag giclée for broadside posters and cards, paired with instant DRM-free **EPUB 3.3** downloads.
2. **Tier 2 — Numbered Collector Runs**: Focused short-run offset / foil-stamped cloth hardcovers and 330gsm black-core gilded collector card decks (*The Golden Deck*, *Classic 54 Triangle Centers*, *Kepler Solstice Gift Box*) fulfilled through dedicated partner warehouses once validated by Tier 1 demand.
