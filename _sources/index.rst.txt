==============
GEOMETOR Press
==============

.. image:: _static/medallion.svg
   :alt: GEOMETOR Press Medallion
   :class: repo-medallion-hero

**GEOMETOR Press** is the editorial, typographic, and artifact-design headquarters of the GEOMETOR archive. It organizes the workspace's scholarly codices, mathematical catalogs, archival folios, and calibrated vector plates into a unified five-spine publishing house—spanning clothbound print quartos, EPUB 3.3 editions, collector card decks, foil stationery, museum broadsides, and compass-and-straightedge author portraits.

Key Features
------------

.. container:: home-feature-grid

   .. container:: home-feature-card

      **48-Repository Readiness Census**

      Track every GEOMETOR codex and catalog in ``resources/shelf.yaml`` across print quartos, EPUB 3.3 editions, collector decks, and broadsides.

   .. container:: home-feature-card

      **Live Parametric SVG Studios**

      Adjust Aldine page ratios, mineral spine pigments, and geometric motifs directly in the browser and export strict XML vector masters.

   .. container:: home-feature-card

      **Unified Codex & SVG Standards**

      Shared specifications for ``.euclid-graphic`` diagrams, theme-adaptive dark/light CSS, ``:layout: book`` navigation, and ``clerk`` scaffolding.
