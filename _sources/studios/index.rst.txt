==============================
Interactive Parametric Studios
==============================

Both parametric SVG design studios live in ``demos/`` as zero-CDN, offline-ready HTML applications and are embedded directly below. Every specimen can be exported on the spot as a clean vector ``.svg`` file.

1. Parametric Cover & Spine System Studio
-----------------------------------------

- **Standalone File**: ``demos/cover_studio.html`` (`Open Fullscreen <../_static/studios/cover_studio.html>`__)
- **Capabilities**: Switch across **8 GEOMETOR titles** spanning all **5 Spine Series**, compare *Aldine Rag*, *Nocturnal Foil*, and *Full-Cloth* bindings, overlay the :math:`\phi` Golden Canon grid, inspect the matched **8-Book Spine Shelf**, and export front covers as SVG.

2. Cards, Stationery & Broadside Poster Studio
----------------------------------------------

- **Standalone File**: ``demos/cards_posters_studio.html`` (`Open Fullscreen <../_static/studios/cards_posters_studio.html>`__)
- **Capabilities**: Click collector study cards to **3D-flip** between uncluttered diagram faces and exact algebraic/proof backs (*The Golden Deck*, *Centers of the Triangle*, *Leonardo's 60 Polyhedra*), inspect folded seasonal greeting cards (*Kepler 1611*, *Feuerbach 1822*, *Phyllotaxis 137.5°*), preview ``24″ × 36″`` vector broadsides, and download card/poster SVGs.
