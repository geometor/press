GEOMETOR Press¶

GEOMETOR Press is the editorial, typographic, and artifact-design headquarters of the GEOMETOR archive. It organizes the workspace’s scholarly codices, mathematical catalogs, archival folios, and calibrated vector plates into a unified five-spine publishing house—spanning clothbound print quartos, EPUB 3.3 editions, collector card decks, foil stationery, museum broadsides, and compass-and-straightedge author portraits.
