GEOMETOR Press¶

Exploring the Architecture of All That Is — Exact Symbolic & Classical Editions.

GEOMETOR Press is the editorial and tactile publishing house of the interconnected GEOMETOR semantic digital archive. It unites 74 book codices, 11 mathematical catalogs, tens of thousands of calibrated vector diagrams, and thousands of archival folios into a cohesive family of fine clothbound quartos, bilingual scholarly editions, collector card decks, and museum broadsides speaking one shared geometric language.

  1. Print Books & Fine Collector Quartos — Trade paperbacks, facing-page bilingual scholarly editions (Greek, Latin, German, French, Italian, and English), and clothbound foil-stamped hardcovers.

  2. EPUB 3.3 Electronic Editions — Reflowable scholarly e-books and fixed-layout polychrome visual editions.

  3. Collector, Study & Puzzle Card Decks — The Golden Deck (52 cards), Centers of the Triangle: Series I (54 cards), Leonardo’s 60 Polyhedra, and Archimedes’ Stomachion.

  4. Folded Foil Greeting & Seasonal Note Cards — Kepler’s De Nive Sexangula (1611), Feuerbach’s Kiss (1822), Golden Meristem (137.5°), and Dürer & Pacioli Majuscules.

  5. Museum-Grade Broadside Posters — 18″ × 24″ and 24″ × 36″ archival cotton rag giclée prints (The Deductive Spine G.1–G.601, Polychrome Euclid Grid, Four Reference Triangles).

  6. Living Instrument Apps — Interactive step-scrubbers, 3D polyhedra/wave-field studios, and playable harmonic scales.
